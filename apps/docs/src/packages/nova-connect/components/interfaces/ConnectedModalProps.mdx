Props for the [ConnectedModal](?path=/docs/packages-nova-connect-components-variables-connectedmodal--docs) component. `appChains` and `solanaRPCUrls` give the chains of the network
screen; `transactionPool`, `pulsarAdapter` and `pagination` (Pulsar) feed the transaction history, whose rows open their
details with `removeTxFromPool`.

### removeTxFromPool?

> `optional` **removeTxFromPool?**: (`txKey`) => `void`

Defined in: [packages/nova-connect/src/hooks/useNovaConnect.ts:50](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-connect/src/hooks/useNovaConnect.ts#L50)

`removeTxFromPool` of the Pulsar store. With it the rows of the history in the connected modal open their details, which
offer to stop tracking a transaction that has been pending for over 10 minutes: the spinning connect button is what a
person acts on when one never ends. An app that shows a pool of its own must remove the record there too.

##### txKey

`string`
