### removeTxFromPool?

> `optional` **removeTxFromPool?**: (`txKey`) => `void`

Defined in: [packages/nova-connect/src/hooks/useNovaConnect.ts:49](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-connect/src/hooks/useNovaConnect.ts#L49)

`removeTxFromPool` of the Pulsar store. With it the rows of the history in the connected modal open their details, which
offer to stop tracking a transaction that has been pending for over 10 minutes: the spinning connect button is what a
person acts on when one never ends. An app that shows a pool of its own must remove the record there too.

### sounds?

> `optional` **sounds?**: `NovaSoundsConfig`

Defined in: [packages/nova-connect/src/hooks/useNovaConnect.ts:62](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-connect/src/hooks/useNovaConnect.ts#L62)

The sounds of Nova Connect, off without it: a wallet connected and disconnected, an error of a connection, a change of
network, a copy and a balance refreshed by hand. `true` plays all, and `{ copy: false, volume: 0.3 }` leaves out one and
sets the volume. They are synthesized in the browser: no files and no requests. See `NovaSoundsConfig` of
`@tuwaio/nova-core/utils` for when they stay silent.
