### motion?

> `optional` **motion?**: `"assemble"`

Defined in: [components/QuasarLoader.tsx:40](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-core/src/components/QuasarLoader.tsx#L40)

`assemble` plays once when the loader appears: the pixel mark assembles from its pixels, each flying in to its place
from a spot nearby (`pixelFlight`, the motion of the icons of `@tuwaio/nova-icons`), and its light runs from the
start. For the mark shown large, as beside the title of a sign-in page. The classic look, and a reader who asks for
less motion, get the mark at once. None by default.

### variant?

Defined in: [components/QuasarLoader.tsx:21](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-core/src/components/QuasarLoader.tsx#L21)

`full` is the whole mark, for 40–72 px. `compact` is a rounder disk and the core, bolder, for 12–24 px: buttons and
