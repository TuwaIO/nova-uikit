# pixelFlight

> `const` **pixelFlight**: (`x`, `y`, `options?`) => `ReturnType`\<*typeof* [`pixelFlight`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-icons-functions-pixelflight--docs)\> = `flightOfNovaIcons`

Defined in: [pixel/flight.ts:17](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-core/src/pixel/flight.ts#L17)

Where a pixel of a picture that assembles itself comes from, and when: `pixelFlight` of `@tuwaio/nova-icons`, the one
motion of every pixel drawing of Nova (the icons, the marks of a result, the pictures of the connect modal). Give each
pixel `data-px` and this `style`, and its parent `data-motion="assemble"`: the pixel flies in to its place from a spot
3 to 7 cells away (times `spread`), after a delay of up to `stagger` ms, once, with a little overshoot. The same pixel
gets the same spot on every render.

## Parameters

### x

`number`

The column of the pixel on its grid.

### y

`number`

The row of the pixel on its grid.

### options?

How far and over how long the pixels come.

#### spread?

`number`

The distance in cells, times this; 1 by default.

#### stagger?

`number`

The longest delay of a pixel, in ms; 220 by default.

## Returns

`ReturnType`\<*typeof* [`pixelFlight`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-icons-functions-pixelflight--docs)\>

The `style` of the pixel: `--nova-px-x`, `--nova-px-y` and `--nova-px-delay`.
