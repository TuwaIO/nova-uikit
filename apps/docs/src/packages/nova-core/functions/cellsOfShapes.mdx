# cellsOfShapes()

> **cellsOfShapes**(`shapes`): [`Cell`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-core-type-aliases-cell--docs)[]

Defined in: [pixel/flight.ts:44](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-core/src/pixel/flight.ts#L44)

The pixels a drawing is made of, read from its shapes: rectangles of whole pixels and paths made of runs of pixels,
which is how the pixel icons and the pixel loader of Nova are drawn. A drawing of lines and curves (the classic look)
has none.

## Parameters

### shapes

[`PixelShape`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-core-type-aliases-pixelshape--docs)[]

The rectangles and the paths of the drawing.

## Returns

[`Cell`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-core-type-aliases-cell--docs)[]

Its pixels, each once.
