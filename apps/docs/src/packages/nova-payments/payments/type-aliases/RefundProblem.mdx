# RefundProblem

> **RefundProblem** = `"notEnough"` \| `"declined"` \| `"unsupported"` \| `"wrongNetwork"` \| `"failed"` \| `"noRecipient"`

Defined in: [hooks/useRefund.ts:47](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/hooks/useRefund.ts#L47)

Why a refund was not sent: the wallet holds too little (`notEnough`), the merchant declined, the wallet cannot send
it or sent it on another network, it failed, or Quasar does not know the payer (`noRecipient`).
