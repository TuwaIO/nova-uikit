Defined in: [hooks/usePayerProof.ts:39](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/hooks/usePayerProof.ts#L39)

Confirms the invoice from the wallet: switches the wallet to the method's network when it is on another one, then
has it sign Quasar's message (`confirmPayer` of the checkout store).

#### Parameters

##### options?

`quote: true` locks the price once the wallet confirmed (the step before the price).

###### quote?

`boolean`

Whether to lock the price after it.

#### Returns

`Promise`\<`boolean`\>

Whether the wallet confirmed the invoice.
