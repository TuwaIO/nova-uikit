# lockPrice()

> **lockPrice**(`store`, `showConfirmWallet`): `Promise`\<`CheckoutQuote` \| `null`\>

Defined in: [utils/payerProof.ts:62](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/payerProof.ts#L62)

Locks the price for the wallet the buyer continued with, or turns to the step that confirms the wallet first: while
the checkout store says the wallet must confirm the invoice (`payerProof` `required`), no price is asked; when Quasar
refuses the price with `payer_proof_required` (a wallet it wants confirmed, which the checkout did not announce), the
step shows too.

Side effects: `POST quote` through the checkout store.

## Parameters

### store

[`LockPriceStore`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-interfaces-lockpricestore--docs)

The checkout store.

### showConfirmWallet

(`show`) => `void`

Shows (`true`) or leaves (`false`) the step that confirms the wallet.

## Returns

`Promise`\<`CheckoutQuote` \| `null`\>

The quote, or `null` when none was locked.
