# usePaymentsLabels()

> **usePaymentsLabels**(`locale`): [`NovaPaymentsLabels`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-i18n-type-aliases-novapaymentslabels--docs)

Defined in: [hooks/usePaymentsLabels.ts:15](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/hooks/usePaymentsLabels.ts#L15)

The labels of a language: the built-in ones (English for a language without its own), then `labels` of
`NovaPaymentsProvider`, then its `customization.labels.transform`. The built-in labels of a language other than
English load on first use (see `loadLabels`); until they arrive the hook keeps the labels it showed before (English
the first time) and renders again once they are loaded.

## Parameters

### locale

`string`

The language of the checkout.

## Returns

[`NovaPaymentsLabels`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-i18n-type-aliases-novapaymentslabels--docs)

The labels.
