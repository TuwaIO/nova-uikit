# needsLeaveConfirm()

> **needsLeaveConfirm**(`phase`, `error?`): `boolean`

Defined in: [utils/outcome.ts:87](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/outcome.ts#L87)

Whether closing the checkout asks first: only while nothing has been sent. Once a transaction is on its way, or the
invoice is settled, the checkout closes without a question; also after a payment that left the wallet, or may have
([leftTheWalletText](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-functions-leftthewallettext--docs): a transaction that does not pay the invoice, a payment Quasar closed without a result on
chain within 24 hours, a transaction another invoice handed over first, a confirmed payment Quasar has not recorded
yet), so the question's "Nothing is sent yet" would not be true.

## Parameters

### phase

`CheckoutPhase`

Where the payment stands.

### error?

`CheckoutError` \| `null`

The store's last error, if any.

## Returns

`boolean`

`true` to ask.
