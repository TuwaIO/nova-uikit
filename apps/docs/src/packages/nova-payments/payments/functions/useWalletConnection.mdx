# useWalletConnection()

> **useWalletConnection**(`onConnectWallet?`): [`WalletConnection`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-interfaces-walletconnection--docs)

Defined in: [hooks/useWalletConnection.ts:32](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/hooks/useWalletConnection.ts#L32)

Connects a wallet for a payment: opens the connect modal of Nova Connect on the wallets of the method's chain family
when the checkout is inside `NovaConnectProvider` of `@tuwaio/nova-connect` (the app's, or Nova's own connection of
[NovaPaymentsProvider](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-functions-novapaymentsprovider--docs)), else calls the app's handler: `onConnectWallet`, or that of `NovaPaymentsProvider`.

## Parameters

### onConnectWallet?

(`family`) => `void`

The app's handler, for apps that connect wallets without Nova Connect; overrides the
`onConnectWallet` of `NovaPaymentsProvider`.

[`WalletConnection`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-interfaces-walletconnection--docs)

See [WalletConnection](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-interfaces-walletconnection--docs).
