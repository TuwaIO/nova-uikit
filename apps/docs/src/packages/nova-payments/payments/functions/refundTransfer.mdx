# refundTransfer()

> **refundTransfer**(`instructions`): `PaymentInstructions` \| `null`

Defined in: [utils/refund.ts:20](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/refund.ts#L20)

The transfer of a refund as a payments adapter sends it (`sendTransfer`): the asset of the payment to the payer, on
the chain of the payment. The token is the contract or mint of the CAIP-19 asset, `native` for the coin of the chain.

## Parameters

### instructions

\{ \}

What Quasar asks to send.

## Returns

`PaymentInstructions` \| `null`

The transfer, or `null` without a recipient or on a chain no adapter pays on.
