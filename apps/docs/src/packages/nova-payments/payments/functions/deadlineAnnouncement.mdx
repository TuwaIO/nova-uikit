# deadlineAnnouncement()

> **deadlineAnnouncement**(`previousMs`, `ms`, `announcements?`): `number` \| `null`

Defined in: [utils/checkout.ts:178](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/checkout.ts#L178)

Whether a tick of the invoice countdown crossed one of the announcement minutes ([DEADLINE\_ANNOUNCEMENTS](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-variables-deadline-announcements--docs) by
default): the checkout announces the time left then (WCAG 2.2.1), not every second.

## Parameters

### previousMs

`number` \| `null`

Time left at the previous tick; `null` on the first one.

### ms

`number`

Time left now.

### announcements?

readonly `number`[] = `DEADLINE_ANNOUNCEMENTS`

The minutes before the deadline to announce at.

## Returns

`number` \| `null`

The minutes to announce (the nearest when several were crossed at once), or `null`.
