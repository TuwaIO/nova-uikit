# restRateText()

> **restRateText**(`quote`, `now`, `labels`, `merchant`, `locale`): `string` \| `null`

Defined in: [utils/outcome.ts:251](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/outcome.ts#L251)

What the buyer is told about the rate of the rest of an underpaid invoice, in an asset that moves against the invoice
currency (`rateHeldUntil` of the quote): until when a payment settles by itself, or that the seller now decides.

## Parameters

### quote

`Pick`\<`CheckoutQuote`, `"rateHeldUntil"`\> \| `null`

The locked quote.

### now

`number`

The time now, in milliseconds.

### labels

[`NovaPaymentsLabels`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-i18n-type-aliases-novapaymentslabels--docs)

The labels of the checkout language.

### merchant

`string`

The seller's name.

### locale

`string`

The language of the checkout.

## Returns

`string` \| `null`

The text, or `null` when the rate holds until the due date.
