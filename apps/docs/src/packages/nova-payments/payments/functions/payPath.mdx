# payPath()

> **payPath**(`quote`, `options`): [`PayPath`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-type-aliases-paypath--docs)

Defined in: [utils/pay.ts:25](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/pay.ts#L25)

How the wallet pays a quote. A smart wallet that takes a paymaster sends sponsored calls; any other wallet signs the
authorization; the buyer may choose to pay the gas instead.

## Parameters

### quote

`CheckoutQuote`

The locked quote.

### options

What the buyer and the wallet allow.

#### canSponsorCalls

`boolean`

The wallet sends `wallet_sendCalls` with the `paymasterService` capability.

#### withGas

`boolean`

The buyer chose "Pay with gas instead".

## Returns

[`PayPath`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-type-aliases-paypath--docs)

The path.
