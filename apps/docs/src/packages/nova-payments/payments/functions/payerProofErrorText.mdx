# payerProofErrorText()

> **payerProofErrorText**(`error`, `problem`, `labels`, `values`): `string` \| `null`

Defined in: [utils/payerProof.ts:106](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/payerProof.ts#L106)

What the step that confirms the wallet says about a refusal, with what to do: the wallet's (the buyer declined, the
wallet cannot sign messages, it failed) or Quasar's (`payer_proof_invalid`, `payer_proof_unavailable`,
`payer_chain_mismatch`, `too_many_payers`, `rate_limited`…). The buyer stays on the step and can try again.

## Parameters

### error

`CheckoutError` \| `null` \| `undefined`

The checkout store's error, if any.

### problem

[`PaymentWalletErrorKind`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-type-aliases-paymentwalleterrorkind--docs) \| `null`

Why the wallet did not sign, when the checkout knows it.

### labels

[`NovaPaymentsLabels`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-i18n-type-aliases-novapaymentslabels--docs)

The labels of the checkout language.

### values

The wallet's name, the method's network and the seller's name.

#### merchant

`string`

The seller's name.

#### network

`string`

The network of the method.

#### wallet

`string`

The wallet's name.

## Returns

`string` \| `null`

The text, or `null` with nothing to say (no error, or `payer_proof_required`, the reason the step shows).
