# payerFor()

> **payerFor**(`wallet`, `method`): `string` \| `null`

Defined in: [utils/methods.ts:112](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/methods.ts#L112)

The payer of a method for the connected wallet: its address on the method's chain. A wallet of the other family
cannot pay it; a wallet on another chain of the family pays after switching (the quote names the method's chain).

## Parameters

### wallet

[`CheckoutWallet`](?path=/docs/packages-nova-payments-payments-interfaces-checkoutwallet--docs) \| `null`

The connected wallet.

### method

`CheckoutMethod`

The method.

## Returns

`string` \| `null`

The CAIP-10 account, or `null`.
