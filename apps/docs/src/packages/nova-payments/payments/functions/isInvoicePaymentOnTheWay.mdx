# isInvoicePaymentOnTheWay()

> **isInvoicePaymentOnTheWay**(`state`, `checkout`): `boolean`

Defined in: [utils/checkout.ts:293](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/utils/checkout.ts#L293)

Whether Quasar reports a payment of the invoice on its way, whoever handed it over (another tab, the page before it
was reloaded, a Solana Pay transfer Quasar found): `invoicePaymentOnTheWay` of the checkout store of a current
`@tuwaio/quasar-sdk`, else, with an older store without the field, the `pendingPayments` of the checkout it read.

## Parameters

### state

`object` & `object`

The state of the checkout store.

### checkout

`CheckoutView` \| `null`

What the checkout token opens; `null` until loaded.

## Returns

`boolean`

Whether a payment of the invoice may still arrive.
