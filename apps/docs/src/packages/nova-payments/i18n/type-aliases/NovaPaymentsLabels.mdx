Defined in: [i18n/types.ts:5](https://github.com/TuwaIO/nova-uikit/blob/main/packages/nova-payments/src/i18n/types.ts#L5)

All texts of the Nova Payments checkout, screen readers' included. `{name}` placeholders are filled by the
